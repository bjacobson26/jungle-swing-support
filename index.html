How to play

Play in landscape. Hold the screen to throw your grappling rope, then release to fly. Collect coins to build your chain and multiplier. Three fruits restore one life, up to three lives.

Remove Ads and restore purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It removes ads and includes one ad-free continue per run. Open Remove Ads or Ad settings from the title, pause, or results screen, then choose Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Purchases and refunds are managed by Apple. For billing help, visit Apple purchase support.

Ads and continues

After the first three minutes of play, an ad may appear after losing a heart while you still have lives remaining. Forced ads are spaced by at least three minutes of active play, including across retries. Try Again starts a new run without an ad. At game over, an optional rewarded continue is offered when available, once per run. Finish the rewarded ad to receive the extra life. If an ad is unavailable, you can keep playing a new run.

Where available, you can revisit Ad Privacy Choices in the ad settings sheet.

Report a problem

Email bjacobson26@me.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, game version, and what happened. A screenshot can help. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

High scores and game settings are saved on your device. Deleting the app may remove them; restoring a purchase does not restore a lost high score.
