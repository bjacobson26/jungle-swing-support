Jungle Swing support

Privacy policy

Jungle Swing: Canopy Adventure · Updated October 8, 2026

Jungle Swing is developed by Bo Jacobson. This policy describes information used by the game, its advertising and purchase providers, and support correspondence.

Game progress

The game does not require a player account. Scores, settings, and a cached Remove Ads entitlement are stored on your device. The game has no player chat or user-generated content. We do not operate a game account server or upload your high scores to our own servers.

Advertising and privacy choices

The game integrates Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to provide ads and manage applicable privacy choices. Google may process IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, device or app-scoped identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and maintain its services.

The game requests non-personalized ads, disables Google's publisher first-party identifier, and does not request Apple's tracking authorization. These choices do not mean ads collect no data. Privacy choices are requested where required; Ad Privacy Choices is available from the ad settings sheet when Google's consent system requires it. Ads can be removed with a one-time purchase.

Learn more about Google's privacy practices and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Purchases

Apple handles payment and billing. The game uses StoreKit to verify the Remove Ads purchase and restore access. The game does not receive your card number or billing credentials. See Apple's privacy policy for its processing of purchases.

Support

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond and investigate the issue, retain it only as needed for support and applicable obligations, and do not use it to build advertising profiles.

Your choices

You can use the available ad privacy controls, purchase Remove Ads, or delete the app to remove its local game data. Device backups and Apple purchase records are managed separately by Apple. You can contact us about support correspondence or privacy requests; Google and Apple also provide controls and request channels for information they process.

This website

The support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information such as IP addresses to serve and secure the site. These pages contain no added analytics or advertising scripts. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, email bjacobson26@me.com. This page will be updated if the game's data practices change.
